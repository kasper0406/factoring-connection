# User-supplied items (parallel session, 2026-10-18): dichotomy, batch smoothness, paradigms 4–6

- **a + bN − (ap + bq) = bφ(N) + (b − a)(p − 1)** (correct). At a = b the collision holds mod N, so a hash table works. This is the Hittmeir 2018 regime: N^{1/4}·exp(−Ω(log N/log log N)) via CRT sieving.
- **Deterministic batch smoothness on Q(x) = (x + ⌊√N⌋)² − N plus F₂ linear algebra** is the quadratic sieve. It is a deterministic algorithm, but its running time and success are unproven; that is the missing lemma in notes/codex_r10_frontier.md §3.
- **Paradigm 4 (mod N² Fermat quotients).** The user's formula a^{N+1} − a^{p+q} ≡ −p·a^{q+1}·q_p(a) (mod p²) is WRONG. The correct form is p·(q−1)·a^{q+1}·q_p(a) (mod p²); the orchestrator checked it numerically for p=101, q=103, a ∈ {2,3,5}. This is Okamoto–Uchiyama / Paillier territory, where inverting is provably as hard as factoring or as composite residuosity. Track D lifts also reduced to order tests.
- **Paradigm 5 (class-group BSGS with hash table):** Shanks 1971 (N^{1/4}, or N^{1/5} under GRH), extended by Schnorr–Lenstra and Lenstra–Pomerance. The growth of Cl(−4kN)[2] is mostly ambiguous classes from k's known primes ("useful ambiguous" caveat). It does not shrink the odd exponent.
- **Paradigm 6 (Stage 3 via chirp-z, or matrix-multiplication MPE):** Harvey §4 already evaluates at powers of α (a geometric progression). Paterson–Stockmeyer's T^{(ω+1)/2} is asymptotically worse than T·log²T. At most a constant-factor gain, and the number of leaves is unchanged.
