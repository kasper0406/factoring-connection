# Registration for the doubling experiment

Written before generating samples, computing coefficients or testing candidates. Independent RNG streams use the decimal seed 2026112600 + bits for bits 32, 48 and 64. At each size draw 128 distinct semiprimes per class, with distinct half-size primes, exact product bit length, and uniform rejection sampling of odd candidates in the required residue class. Order the primes by size. Ground truth uses the sampled primes. Retain every accepted row. No split exclusions from the T0 tables.

**Exact baseline.** Given only N, factor it with FLINT, then compute the binomial in both prime fields by Lucas with ordinary or fast modular factorials, and CRT. Compare recovered factors to the labels only afterwards. Independently compare the binomial with Python integer binomials for all distinct odd prime pairs of equal class with product below 1500. For Gaussian prime traces use the unique signed odd a congruent to 1 modulo 4 and b even positive. Check the Gauss formula and both cross terms on split-class samples. This baseline costs more than polynomial in the input bit length. Log every proper gcd from a feature separately from baseline factorization.

**Public features without B.** Evaluate ordinary central binomials at m modulo the small primes 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17 and 31 by Lucas. Evaluate the root-of-unity local carry 2(m mod d) >= d for d = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32. Evaluate Lucas U at m and 2m for (P,Q)=(3,1),(1,-1),(4,1) by matrix powering. Score zero, nonunit, proper gcd and each Jacobi sign for every Lucas endpoint. Score each small-prime zero and each root carry. Record exact class agreement for each binary predicate, both orientations. A public exact candidate must have zero errors at every size and survive proof. A candidate for statistical follow-up needs the same direction and two-sided Fisher p < 0.01/(3 times the number of endpoints) at every size.

**Features after B is supplied.** For the canonical residue in [0,N), take zero, nonunit, proper gcd, upper half, each Jacobi value and each residue bucket modulo 2,3,4,5,7,8,16. Score agreement with T0. Within 1,1 compare the Jacobi and residue indicators against a_p<0, a_q<0, a_p*a_q<0 and 2*a_s^2>s for s=p,q. Same replicated Bonferroni/Fisher rule, correcting for all feature-target pairs. These tests address the finite bank only. The hypotheses are exactness for B=0, no exact pre-B surrogate, and no replicable hidden-data association in this bank.

**Small structural checks.** For equal-class odd prime pairs with N<400, compute the pure E4 coefficient of normalized E_(N-1) over the rationals; report denominator gcds and numerator zero mismatches separately. Check the reflected endpoint relation, and the cyclotomic gcd degree by enumerating the relevant divisors on small pairs. A single valid counterexample rejects an exact substitution. Distinguish a singular denominator from a wrong value. Additional theoretical identities may be proved independently of the experiment.

Before sample generation, add a deterministic balanced-input control. Let L be the lower bound for the half-size primes. Evaluate the product of all integers in [L,2L) congruent to 3 mod 4, directly modulo N using monic polynomial blocks. Its gcd must equal N in 3,3 and 1 in 1,1 with zero exceptions. This control has exponential-in-bit-length cost. Time and verify it on the first four rows per class and size, and independently check it by ordinary multiplication on the 32-bit subset. It is a timing/control construction outside the polylog feature bank.
