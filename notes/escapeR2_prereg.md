# escapeR2: pre-registration of E1–E3

Agent `escapeR2`, phase 13, 2026-10-20. Frozen before any run. All three are **exact checks** of statements that are
either theorems (E1, E3) or model computations (E2). None of them tests an asymptotic claim about factoring. None
proves anything about relation supply. They exist to catch errors in the notes' algebra and arithmetic.

Code (to be written after this file is committed, and frozen at the commit that first runs it):
- `src/escapeR2/transfer.py`: F₂ parity-transfer spaces for subfields of a Galois field, computed from
  permutation groups (E1).
- `src/escapeR2/sizemodel.py`: exact rational size-model floors (E2).
- `src/escapeR2/resplit.py`: the square-coset re-split obstruction on a toy modulus (E3). It is a **harness**: it
  uses the factors of the toy modulus to build an adversarial target set. Nothing in it is operational.
- Tests: `tests/test_escapeR2.py`. Raw output: `experiments/raw/escapeR2_e{1,2,3}.json`.

## E1. Cross-field parity transfer (checks Lemma TI)

**Setting.** L/Q Galois with group G. Subfields K_j = L^{H_j}. An unramified rational prime ℓ with decomposition
group D = ⟨φ⟩ (cyclic). Primes of L above ℓ ↔ left cosets gD. Primes of K_j above ℓ ↔ double cosets H_j g D. The
extension map ι_j sends a prime of K_j to the indicator (over F₂) of the cosets gD in its double coset. Put
Im_j := ι_j(F₂^{H_j\G/D}). The constant function is in every Im_j (it is the image of the rational prime ℓ).

**Quantities.** For two fields, τ₂ := dim(Im_1 ∩ Im_2) − dim F₂^{⟨H_1,H_2⟩\G/D}. For J fields,
τ := dim(Im_1 ∩ Σ_{j≥2} Im_j) − 1 (the transfer dimension of K_1 beyond the rational line).

**Criterion (hypothesis of Lemma TI).** Put H′ := ∩_{j≥2} H_j (its fixed field is the compositum K_2⋯K_J) and
H₁⁺ := ⟨H_1, H′⟩ (fixed field K_1 ∩ K_2⋯K_J). Lemma TI claims dim(Im_1 ∩ Σ_{j≥2} Im_j) ≤ dim F₂^{H₁⁺\G/D}; in
particular τ = 0 when H₁⁺ = G, i.e. when K_1 ∩ K_2⋯K_J = Q.

**Cases.**
- (a) product groups: G = S_3 × S_3, S_3 × S_4, S_3 × S_3 × S_3, with H_j = (point stabiliser in factor j) × (all
  other factors); every cyclic D = ⟨g⟩, g ∈ G (one representative per conjugacy class).
- (b) V₄ = C₂ × C₂ with its three subgroups of order 2 (the three quadratic subfields of a biquadratic field), D trivial.
- (c) a census: G ∈ {S_4, D_4, A_4, C₂³, S_3 × C₂}, all pairs and triples of proper non-trivial subgroups up to
  conjugacy as produced by the code's enumeration (subgroups generated by ≤ 2 elements), D ∈ {trivial, ⟨g⟩ for one
  g per conjugacy class}.

**Predictions (fixed now).**
- P1: dim(Im_1 ∩ Σ_{j≥2} Im_j) ≤ dim F₂^{H₁⁺\G/D} in every case of (a)–(c); hence τ = 0 in every case of (a), and
  in every case of (c) with H₁⁺ = G.
- P2: in (b), τ = 1 (a prime of Q(√ab) is congruent mod 2 to a sum of primes of Q(√a) and Q(√b)).
- P3: τ₂ = 0 in every two-field case of (a)–(c) (Im_1 ∩ Im_2 is exactly the image of K_1 ∩ K_2).
- P4 (descriptive, no prediction): the fraction of (c) triples with τ > 0.

## E2. Size-model floors (checks Prop R3(C) and the level thresholds)

Exponent arithmetic in powers of D (log log factors dropped, as in recurse §3.3–3.4). Chain sub-model: one field of
degree D^δ, log H_f = log H_g = log N/(d+1); a rational root target at exponent 1 − α; r algebraic levels
ζ_1, …, ζ_r inside the same field with δ + ζ_j ≤ α + ζ_{j+1}; terminal δ + ζ_r ≤ 2α; rational sides B-smooth
(1 − δ ≤ 2α); component size exponent rδ + (1 − α) − ζ_r; budget: component size ≤ d^C. The code solves, for each
r ≤ 40 and each C on a rational grid, the exact minimum α (bisection on α with exact rational feasibility tests of
the linear constraints in δ, to tolerance 10⁻⁹, plus closed forms).

**Predictions.**
- Q1: without a budget (C = ∞) the floor for r algebraic levels is (r + 2)/(3r + 5) (r = 0: 2/5; r = 1: 3/8;
  r = 2: 4/11), decreasing to 1/3.
- Q2: α*(C = 1) = 2/5 (budget d: no gain).
- Q3: α*(3/2) = 3/8, and α*(C) = (r + 2)/(3r + 5) whenever C ≥ r + 1; α*(C) → 1/3 as C → ∞; α*(C) > 1/3 for
  every finite C.
- Q4: at α = 3/8 (r = 1, δ = 1/4) the first-level algebraic-children count exponent is 3/8 (codex escapeR2_1 §11).

## E3. Square-coset re-split obstruction (checks Prop RS(ii))

Toy N = pq (40–48 bits, seeds 1–5, `src/common/instances.balanced_semiprime`). Window primes in (Y, 2Y] with
Y = 2^{⌈n/(2k)⌉ − 1}, k = 2. Target set G := window primes that are squares modulo both p and q (harness). S := products
of k distinct primes of G in the top window. For x a unit that is a non-square modulo p, count coprime pairs
(u, v) ∈ S² with u ≡ x·v·□ (mod N); and draw 2000 uniform s, Gauss-reduce x·s² mod N, and record whether the reduced
vector lies in S × S.

**Predictions.**
- R1: the pair count is exactly 0 and the hit count is exactly 0 for every seed (non-square x).
- R2: for x = 1 the pair count is ≥ |S|²/(2·2^{ω(N)})·(1 − 2k³/|G|)… i.e. ≥ |S|²/(2h) (Lemma S″ step 3), whenever
  |G| ≥ 4k³h.

Deviations, if any, will be disclosed in the results note.
