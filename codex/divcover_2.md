**C3 (HS-r incidence inequality).** Let P be a finite set of primes, v = |P| ≥ 2, and E a finite set of nonzero integers such that every pair {p,q} ⊂ P divides a common e ∈ E, and no e ∈ E is divisible by all of P. Blocks B_e = {p∈P : p|e}; Δ = max_p #{e : p | e}; μ = max_{e≠e'} |B_e ∩ B_{e'}| (≤ max_{e≠e'} ω_P(e − e')). Then v ≤ 1 + Δ(μΔ − 1). (Proof: fix B_e, pick w ∉ B_e; each q ∈ B_e co-occurs with w in some e' ≠ e, so |B_e| ≤ μ·Δ; then fix u: P∖{u} ⊆ ∪_{e∋u}(B_e∖{u}).) For a rank-1 AP with p ∤ c for p∈P and length < min pq this gives He–Sahai's Lemma 2.1 (μ = 1). For a GAP with box Π L_k, height H, longest side L_max and band P ⊂ [a√n, √n] (p ∤ generator of the long side): Δ ≤ (L/L_max)·⌈L_max/p⌉ and μ ≤ log(2H)/log(a√n). Claim: with these worst-case bounds the inequality gives, for every α < 1/2 and any rank ≥ 2, nothing stronger than the trivial mass bound L ≥ n^{1−α−o(1)}; the two independent reasons rank ≥ 2 escapes are (a) μ > 1 and (b) Δ ≈ L/L_max for balanced boxes even when μ = 1.
