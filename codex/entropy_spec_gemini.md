## 0. Design Premise & Core Adversarial Warnings for the Parallel Pipeline

Given $N = pq$, the smaller prime factor $p$ has zero Shannon entropy $H(p \mid N) = 0$ yet (if factoring is hard) full computational pseudoentropy. While classical hardcore-bit reductions (ACGS 1988; Goldreich–Levin 1989; Håstad–Näslund 2004) amplify $1/\mathrm{poly}(n)$ advantage when a group action moves the secret, factoring lacks a known polynomial-time factor-moving self-reduction (`notes/factorbits_results.md` Lemma PSR; `notes/selfred_results.md` Theorems SR1, SR2), except on public-mask / cofactor-embedded instances (`Prop GLM`) or $\varepsilon n$ adaptive predicates (Maurer 1995). Nevertheless, **any** $N$-only $\mathrm{poly}(n)$-time Boolean test $T(N) \in \{0,1\}$ achieving persistent advantage $|\Pr[T(N) = Y(p,q)] - 1/2| \ge c \cdot n^{-k}$ beyond the public floor and without splitting $N$ would breach every proved model barrier (`PT`, `PT-X`, `NE1`, `PS`, `D2`, `PT-J`, `AC0-ε`) and establish a fourth computational leak mechanism beyond **(M)** group-order/smoothness, **(A)** archimedean/Diophantine proximity, and **(Q)** quadratic genus.

**Five immediate adversarial warnings against Codex's baseline design (`codex/entropy_codex.md`):**
1. **Universe exhaustion & prime-reuse explosion at small $n$ (`spec_audit_numbers.json`):** Under `src/common/instances.py::balanced_semiprime` (`top2=True`, $p,q \in [3\cdot 2^{b-2}, 2^b)$), $n=24$ ($b=12$) has **only 125 primes and 7,750 distinct semiprimes total** (even full $(2^{11}, 2^{12})$ has 255 primes / 32,385 pairs). Drawing $3 \times 2\cdot 10^5 = 6\cdot 10^5$ "fresh" seeds at $n=24$ repeats every semiprime ~77 times across Discovery/Validation/Confirmation! At $n=32$ ($b=16$: 1,491 primes, $1.11\cdot 10^6$ pairs), 23% of $N$ collide across splits and each prime is reused **805 times** (design effect up to 65 in `leakmap` §5.2); at $n=40$, each prime is reused **63 times**. Splits **must** partition the *prime* universe $P_{\mathrm{disc}} \sqcup P_{\mathrm{val}} \sqcup P_{\mathrm{conf}}$ via hash of $p,q$, and $n \le 48$ requires two-way dyadic cluster-robust variance.
2. **Power impossibility of $1/n^2$ on $m = 2\cdot 10^5$ at $n=96$:** At $n=96$, $\delta = 1/n^2 = 1.085\times 10^{-4}$, whereas $\mathrm{SE}(\hat\delta) = 1/(2\sqrt{2\cdot 10^5}) = 1.118\times 10^{-3}$. Expected $z$ is **$0.097$** (1-sided $\alpha=0.01$ power **$1.29\%$** vs $1.00\%$ null!). Detecting $\delta = 1/96^2$ at $80\%$ power requires **$m = 2.13\times 10^8$** even for **one** pre-locked survivor ($8.41\times 10^8$ for $2\cdot 10^5$-pair de-novo discovery). Meanwhile, plug-in mutual information $\hat I(X;Y)$ has null bias $+1/(2m) = 2.5\times 10^{-6}$ nats at $m=2\cdot 10^5$, **106$\times$ larger** than the true signal $2\delta^2 = 2.35\times 10^{-8}$ nats.
3. **Half-normal noise-floor trap in $\log|\hat\delta(n)|$ scaling fits:** Fitting $\log|\hat\delta(n)|$ vs $\log n$ on $m=2\cdot 10^5$ misclassifies a pure $N^{-1/4} = 2^{-n/4}$ Lehman/SQUFOF exponential leak as polynomial ($k \approx 4.17$) **$98.4\%$ of the time** because $\mathbb{E}|\hat\delta(n)|$ floors at $\sqrt{2/\pi}\,\mathrm{SE} \approx 8.9\times 10^{-4}$ for $n \ge 56$. Scaling **must** be fit in signed linear advantage space by weighted least squares / exact profile likelihood.
4. **Sorting bias $p < q$ on raw factor bits:** Even for uniform $U_1, U_2 \in [0,1]$, $Z = \min(U_1, U_2)$ has linear PDF $2(1-z)$ whose $j$-th MSB satisfies $\Pr[\mathrm{bit}_j(Z)=1] = 1/2 - 2^{-(j+1)}$ (at $n=24$ full range: $0.239, 0.368, 0.436$ for $j=1,2,3$). Uncentered $\Pr[\text{test}=\text{target}]-1/2$ hands constant-$0$ tests a persistent $O(1)$ advantage at fixed offset $j$ from the top!
5. **Linear vs log-linear vs exact PNT size normalization:** Given $N=pq$, $p \in [p_{\min}(N), \sqrt{N})$ with $p_{\min}(N) = \max(B_{\mathrm{lo}}, N/B_{\mathrm{hi}})$. Linear normalization $(p - p_{\min})/(\sqrt{N} - p_{\min}) \ge 1/2$ leaves an **$O(1)$ archimedean bias ($0.0431$ at $n=96$)**; naive log-linear normalization $1 - \ln(q/p)/\ln(N/p_{\min}^2) \ge 1/2$ omits the $1/(\ln p \ln q)$ PNT density curvature, leaving an exact **$\Theta(1/n^2)$ size bias** ($1.36\times 10^{-5}$ at midpoint $N$, steeper across $N$-quantiles). Only the exact PNT probability integral transform $U_{\mathrm{PNT}}(p \mid N)$ + $N$-top-bit stratification removes size info cleanly.

## 1. Rich Typed Test Grammar Beyond Codex's Starting List

Codex's baseline grammar covers depth-1 $x^N, x^{(N-1)/2}, x^{N\pm 1} \bmod N$, $V_N(P,Q)$, binomial 1-shift $(x^N+c)^N$, basic floors/irrationals, prefix CFs of $\sqrt{N}$ and $N/2^k$, and Jacobi/bit/Boolean readouts. Most of that space is already **provably null** (`NE1`, `PS`, `PT-J`, `AC0-ε`) or **bounded at good primes** (`D2`). To probe genuinely open territory across **(M)**, **(A)**, **(Q)** and hypothetical fourth mechanisms, extend to a **typed, gcd-instrumented, cost-capped DAG grammar** ($\le 50\,\mu\mathrm{s}$/eval at $n=96$, enumerating $2\cdot 10^4$–$10^5$ canonical non-degenerate tests after 64-instance truth-table deduplication):

### Types & GCD Instrumentation
- **Types:** `PubInt` (integer SLPs in $N$), `ModN` (elements of $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$), `ExtModN` (elements of $(\mathbb{Z}/N)[T]/(f(T))$, $\deg f \in \{2,3,4,5,r\}$), `Form` (reduced binary quadratic forms $(a,b,c)$ of $\Delta = \pm 4kN$ or $1-4kN$), `ECPt` (projective points $(X:Z)$ on $E(\mathbb{Z}/N)$), `LatOut` (reduced small-lattice invariants), `Bit` ($\{0,1\}$).
- **Mandatory GCD/Split Accumulator:** Every `ModN`/`ExtModN`/`Form`/`ECPt`/`LatOut` operation updates per-instance flags `gcd_split` ($1 < \gcd(v, N) < N$ on any intermediate denominator, zero-test, $Q_i$ root, or lattice vector) without aborting; operational tests never branch on factors, returning deterministic fallback bit $0$ on singular inversions while logging the split.

### Seven Extension Families (`G1`–`G7`)

1. **`G1` — Open-Frontier `PRIM` Nodes (Targeting Exact Breakdowns in `notes/prim_results.md` §5–§7):**
   - *Multi-term inner depth-2 & summed nested gates* (escapes `Theorem D2`; Weil degree $\sim p$): $(x^{E_1(N)} + y^{E_2(N)} + c)^{E_3(N)} \bmod N$ and $(x^N + c_1)^N \pm (y^N + c_2)^N \bmod N$ for small independent prime bases $x,y \in \{2,3,5,7\}$, $c_i \in \{\pm 1, \pm 2, 3\}$.
   - *Shift-depth $\ge 2$ towers with non-torsion targets* (escapes unit-exponent small-order peeling `prim` §5, `FROZEN` $|t|\le 2$, and `CLOSURE`): $(((x^N + c_1)^N + c_2)^N - y^N) \bmod N$ and $((x^{2N+1} + c_1)^N + c_2)^N - c_3 \bmod N$ ($c_3 \in \{2,3,5\}$).
   - *Modular-inverse / rational-map gates* (probes Shparlinski Open Problem 8 exponential Kloosterman sums $\sum e_p(a g^{1/s} + b w^s)$): $((x^N + c_1)^{-1} + c_2)^N \bmod N$ and Rédei/Chebyshev rational maps composed with $N$-gates.
   - *SLP-compressed exponents & bases* (defeats `D2`'s GRH order hypothesis — $y = 2^{\mathrm{lcm}(1..B)}$ makes 79% of 16-bit primes exceptional — and probes `slpcirc` open $\tau$-type root density & `J1`'s height barrier): $M_B = \prod_{\ell \le B} \ell^{\lfloor \log_\ell B \rfloor}$ ($B \in \{16, 32, 64\}$) in $((x^N + 1)^{M_B} + c)^N \bmod N$, Jacobi $((x^N + c)^{M_B} - 1 \mid N)$, and pure/gate compressed Jacobi $(2^{2^k} + c \mid N)$, $(P_{\mathrm{SLP}}(x^N) \mid N)$ ($k \in \{6..10\}$).
   - *Higher-degree extension algebras & coordinate readouts* (`leakmap` §3.5, `prim` §2 OPEN): asymmetric Lucas $U_N(P,Q) \bmod N$, cubic/quartic Frobenius trace and individual coordinates in $(\mathbb{Z}/N)[T]/(T^d - aT - b)$ ($d \in \{3,4\}$), and AKS-quotient monomial coefficients $\mathrm{coeff}_j((T+a)^N \bmod (N, T^r - 1))$ for $r \in \{5,7,11,13\}$ (probes short-interval incomplete sums over $\mathbb{F}_{p^d}^*$ where $\mathrm{ord} \gg |I|$).

2. **`G2` — Class Group $\mathrm{Cl}(-4kN)$ & Discriminant $-N / -4kN$ Form Arithmetic (`notes/genusleak_results.md`, `classE`):**
   - For multipliers $k \in \{1,2,3,5,7\}$ and the first 4 primes $\ell \ge 3$ with $(-4kN \mid \ell) = +1$ (public from $N \bmod 4k\ell$), construct prime forms $f_\ell = (\ell, b_\ell, (b_\ell^2 + kN)/(4\ell))$ via mod-$\ell$ square roots ($O(1)$ cost).
   - **Non-trivial reduction regime** (fixing `classE` §5's uninformative $\ell^j < \sqrt{N}$ bug): evaluate Gauss-reduced forms via $O(n)$ NUCOMP/squaring steps at exponents **exceeding $\sqrt{N}$**:
     - `PRIM`-in-$\mathrm{Cl}(-4kN)$: $f_\ell^N$, $f_\ell^{N \pm 1}$, $f_\ell^{\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor}$, $f_\ell^{M_{32} \cdot 2^{\lceil n/2 \rceil}}$, and short products $f_{\ell_1}^{e_1} f_{\ell_2}^{e_2}$ ($\prod \ell_i^{e_i} > 2N^{1/2}$). Note $h(-4kN) \approx \frac{\sqrt{4kN}}{\pi} L(1, \chi_{-4kN})$ couples $N$ to $L(1,\chi)$ rather than $p-1$.
   - Extract: $(a_{\mathrm{red}} \mid N)$, $(a_{\mathrm{red}} \mid \ell')$, $\chi_{-4}(a_{\mathrm{red}})$, $\chi_8(a_{\mathrm{red}})$, $\mathrm{sgn}(b_{\mathrm{red}})$, $\lfloor 2a_{\mathrm{red}} / \sqrt{4kN} \rfloor$, and proximity $[|b_{\mathrm{red}}| \le a_{\mathrm{red}}/4]$ to ambiguous classes.

3. **`G3` — Multiplier-Pencil CFs $\sqrt{kN}$, Real Infrastructure Giant Steps & Class-E Structured Rationals (`leakmap` §5, `newfamily`, `classE`):**
   - *Multiplier pencil CFs* of $\sqrt{kN}$ ($k \in \{1,2,3,5,6,7,11,13\}$, prefix $T \in \{16, 64\}$): counts of odd/even $i$ where squarefree core of $Q_i$ divides $2\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot 7$ (the exact `((−1)^i Q_i | p) = 1` genus channel), $\max \log_2 a_i$, Gauss–Kuzmin empirical KL, and running Kronecker products $\prod_{i \le T} (Q_i \mid \ell)$.
   - *Real infrastructure giant steps in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{kN})$*: $O(n)$-step Shanks NUCOMP jump to regulator distance $D \in \{\lfloor N^{1/4} \rfloor, \lfloor N^{1/3} \rfloor, \lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor, N\}$, reading $(Q_D \mid N)$, $\mathrm{sgn}(P_D - \sqrt{kN}/2)$, and $[Q_D < \sqrt{kN}/2]$.
   - *Class-E structured Euclid/CF functionals at moving numerators* $a(N) \in \{\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor, N^{-1} \bmod \lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor, 2^N \bmod N, 2^{(N-1)/2} \bmod N, (2^{(N-1)/2} \bmod N)^{-1} \bmod N\}$ (probing `classE`'s two open zones: midpoint $s_k \in [N^{7/12}, N^{3/4}]$ and reversed half $\bar a/N$):
     - Exact Rademacher integer $\Phi(a/N) = (a + \bar a)/N - 12 s(a,N) \bmod m$ ($m \in \{2,3,4,8\}$), CF length parity, alternating partial-quotient sum $\sum (-1)^i a_i$, midpoint power-residue symbol $((-1)^k s_k r_{k+1} \mid N)$ at first $s_k \ge \sqrt{N}$, Manin modular symbols $\{\infty, a(N)/N\} \in H_1(X_0(M), \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})$ ($M \in \{11, 14, 15, 17\}$ via $O(n)$ $\mathrm{SL}_2$ tracking), and non-telescoping 3D shifted cotangent/floor sums (`classE` §3.5, shift $(N-1)/2$).

4. **`G4` — Modular-Form, `X_0(N)`, and Torsion-Algebra Cusp Coefficient Proxies (`geometry`, `nativemod`, `cuspcoef`):**
   - *`#X_0(N)(\mathbb{F}_\ell)` discriminant fibers* (`geometry` §0): for $\ell \in \{2,3,5,7,11\}$, Frobenius symbols $((t^2 - 4\ell \mid N))_{|t| < 2\sqrt{\ell}}$ combined with norm-torus Frobenius steps $x^{N \pm 1} \bmod (N, x^2 - tx + \ell)$ at non-scalar $t$.
   - *Edixhoven–Couveignes composite torsion-algebra readouts* (`nativemod` §0 Q1 OPEN): for fixed non-CM curves $E \in \{\text{11a1}, \text{14a1}, \text{37a1}\}$ and $\ell \in \{3,5,7\}$, literal Frobenius $X_N(x) = x^N \bmod (N, \psi_\ell(x))$ in $A_\ell = (\mathbb{Z}/N)[x]/(\psi_\ell(x))$: trace $\mathrm{Tr}_{A_\ell}(X_N^j)$, resultant/Jacobi $(\mathrm{Res}_x(\psi_\ell, X_N - x) \mid N)$ (gcd-guarded!).

## 2. Targets: Exact Size-Free Top/Ratio Bits, Safe GL Parities, and Sanity Suite

Define $N = pq$ with $p = \min(p,q) < \sqrt{N} < q$, primes uniform in $(B_{\mathrm{lo}}, B_{\mathrm{hi}}]$ (recommend testing **both** `instances.py` `top2=True` $[3\cdot 2^{b-2}, 2^b)$ and full $(2^{b-1}, 2^b)$ without post-filtering $N \ge 2^{2b-1}$, or explicitly recording $[B_{\mathrm{lo}}, B_{\mathrm{hi}}]$). Every target must be paired with its **randomly-oriented twin** $p_* \in_R \{p, q\}$ (independent harness coin flip) to separate symmetric factor properties from $p < q$ orientation!

1. **Exact Size-Free Top Bits of $p$ & Ratio Bits of $p/q$ (via PNT Probability Integral Transform):**
   - Given $N$, the exact support of $p$ is $[p_{\min}(N), \sqrt{N})$ with $p_{\min}(N) = \max(B_{\mathrm{lo}}, N/B_{\mathrm{hi}})$ and $s_{\min}(N) = \ln p_{\min}(N)$.
   - Under PNT prime density $f(u) \propto 1/\ln u$, change of variables $(p, q) \mapsto (s = \ln p, N = pq)$ yields Jacobian $dq = dN/p$ and $dp/p = ds$, so the conditional density of $s = \ln p$ given $N$ on $[s_{\min}(N), \frac{1}{2}\ln N)$ is **exactly proportional to $\frac{1}{s(\ln N - s)}$**, whose closed-form cumulative distribution (PIT) is:
     $$U_{\mathrm{PNT}}(p \mid N) = 1 - \frac{\ln(\ln q / \ln p)}{\ln\!\big((\ln N - s_{\min}(N)) / s_{\min}(N)\big)} \in [0, 1)$$
     (computed in 128-bit `gmpy2.mpfr` precision; drop degenerate boundary instances where $\sqrt{N} - p_{\min}(N) < 16 \ln N$, probability $O(\ln N / X)$).
   - **Size-free top/ratio bit targets:** $T_{\mathrm{top}, k}(p \mid N) := \lfloor 2^k U_{\mathrm{PNT}}(p \mid N) \rfloor \bmod 2$ for $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$. Note $U_{\mathrm{PNT}}$ simultaneously normalizes **both** top bits of $p$ and ratio bits of $p/q$ because $q = N/p$ makes $p/q = p^2/N$ strictly monotone in $p$ at fixed $N$!
   - *Why not linear or log-linear?* As verified in `spec_audit_numbers.json`, linear $(p - p_{\min})/(\sqrt{N}-p_{\min}) \ge 1/2$ has **$0.0431$ ($O(1)$) size bias** at $n=96$, and log-linear $1 - \ln(q/p)/\ln(N/p_{\min}^2) \ge 1/2$ has **$\Theta(1/n^2)$ bias** from neglecting $1/(\ln p \ln q)$! As belt-and-suspenders, **also** cross-fit isotonic/quantile empirical CDF $\hat F_{\mathrm{train}}(p \mid \text{bin}_{4096}(N))$ on Discovery and stratify all tests on the top 10 bits of $N$.

2. **Sanitized Goldreich–Levin (`GL`) Parities $\langle r, p \rangle \bmod 2$:**
   - **Mask sanitation (mandatory):** Force $r_0 = 0$ (since $p_0 \equiv 1$) and $r_{b-1} = r_{b-2} = \dots = r_{b-k_{\mathrm{top}}} = 0$ ($k_{\mathrm{top}} = \max(4, \lceil \log_2 n \rceil)$), and **require $\mathrm{popcount}(r_{[1, \lfloor b/2 \rfloor]}) \ge 4$**. Without low-half support, a random mask lands purely in the top $k$ bits with probability $2^{-k}$ and leaks $N$'s archimedean size at rate $\Theta(2^{-k})$!
   - **Two GL tracks:**
     - *`GL-fixed`* ($M_{\mathrm{GL}} = 8$ deterministic SHAKE-128 masks $r^{(1)}, \dots, r^{(8)}$ per $n$, derived from public string `"GL|n"`, evaluated on both $p_{\min}$ and random $p_*$), PLUS $4$ **PIT-GL parities** $\langle r, \lfloor 2^{16} U_{\mathrm{PNT}}(p \mid N) \rfloor \rangle \bmod 2$ testing dense parities of size-free top bits.
     - *`GL-var`* (instance-varying public $r_i$): included ONLY for two-input tests $T(N, r_i)$ (e.g., `Prop GLM` control $M = N \cdot (r_i N^{-1} \bmod 2^{b-1})$), since an $r_i$-oblivious $T(N)$ has exact zero population correlation with $\langle r_i, p \rangle$.

3. **Interior, Low-Bit Sanity, Genus, and Modular Targets:**
   - *Middle bits:* $\mathrm{bit}_{\lfloor b/2 \rfloor}(p)$, $\mathrm{bit}_{\lfloor b/2 \rfloor + 1}(p)$, $\mathrm{bit}_{\lfloor b/3 \rfloor}(p)$ (evaluated with stratum centering to kill the $2^{-(j+1)}$ sorting offset).
   - *Low-bit sanity controls (must be exact null after $N \bmod 2^{k+2}$ stratification):* $\mathrm{bit}_1(p)$ ($p \equiv 3 \bmod 4$ on $N \equiv 3 \bmod 4$, orientation bit; on $N \equiv 1 \bmod 4$ this IS $-\varepsilon(p)$, so keep $N \equiv 1,3 \bmod 4$ separate!), $\mathrm{bit}_2(p)$, $\mathrm{bit}_3(p)$, $\mathrm{bit}_4(p)$.
   - *Genus / Quadratic characters (evaluated strictly on the ambiguous/same-sign fiber $(D \mid N) = +1$ where $(D \mid p) = (D \mid q)$):* $\varepsilon(p) = (-1 \mid p)$ on $N \equiv 1 \pmod 4$; $(2 \mid p)$ on $N \equiv 1 \pmod 8$; $((-3) \mid p)$, $(5 \mid p)$, $((-7) \mid p)$, $((-11) \mid p)$ on $(D \mid N) = +1$. (On $(D \mid N) = -1$, $(D \mid p_{\min})$ is a pure orientation target — track separately!)
   - *Non-genus higher characters & balanced $p \bmod m$:* cubic $[p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod 7]$ on $N \equiv 1 \pmod 7$; quartic $[p \equiv 1 \pmod 5]$ on $N \equiv 1 \pmod 5$ restricted to $p,q \equiv \pm 1 \pmod 5$ (`leakmap` NG1, NG2); balanced half-set $[p \bmod 3 = 1]$ and $[p \bmod 5 \in \{1,2\}]$.

## 3. Statistics Protocol: Multiple Testing, $n=96$ Power, and Polynomial vs Exponential Decay

### 3A. Stratum-Centered Advantage, Variance, and Bias-Corrected Mutual Information
Partition instances by public floor stratum $h = H(N) := (N \bmod M_0,\; \lfloor 2^{10}(N - N_{\min})/(N_{\max}-N_{\min}) \rfloor)$ with $M_0 = 2^8 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 3840$ (collapsing strata with $m_h < 8$ into coarse $(N \bmod 256, \text{top}_6(N))$).
- **Stratum-centered advantage:** $\widehat{\mathrm{adv}}_\perp = \frac{2}{m}\sum_h \sum_{i \in h} (X_i - \bar X_h)(Y_i - \bar Y_h)$. When $\bar X_h = \bar Y_h = 1/2$, $\widehat{\mathrm{adv}}_\perp \equiv \widehat{\Pr}[X=Y] - 1/2 = \delta$ identically, while **eliminating** base-rate imbalances $(\bar Y_h \ne 1/2)$ and public size/CRT leaks!
- **Exact stratified & dyadic-cluster z-scores:**
  - For $n \ge 56$ (prime reuse $< 0.35$): Mantel–Haenszel exact permutation variance $V_{\mathrm{MH}} = \sum_h \frac{S_{XX,h} S_{YY,h}}{m_h - 1}$, $Z = U / \sqrt{V_{\mathrm{MH}}}$ (`src/eps/analysis.py::strat_z`), plus exact Clopper–Pearson / stratified Newcombe CIs on $\widehat{\mathrm{adv}}_\perp$. Note $Z$ scales by $\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}$, automatically giving full power to rare high-purity certificates ($\Pr[X=1] \ll 1/2$) whose unconditional $|\Pr[X=Y]-1/2|$ is small!
  - For $n \le 48$: **two-way dyadic cluster-robust variance** $V_{\mathrm{dyad}} = \sum_{p} (\sum_{i \ni p} e_i)^2 - \sum_{i} e_i^2$ on centered residuals $e_i = (X_i - \bar X_h)(Y_i - \bar Y_h)$ (`leakmap` v2), accounting for shared primes across pairs.
- **Cross-split / Miller-corrected Mutual Information:** Never threshold raw plug-in $\hat I(X;Y)$ ($\mathbb{E}_{H_0}[\hat I] \approx \frac{1}{2m}$ nats $= 106\times$ true $1/96^2$ signal at $m=2\cdot 10^5$). Report Miller–Madow corrected $\hat I_{\mathrm{BC}} = \hat I - \frac{\mathrm{df}}{2 m \ln 2}$ **and** zero-mean out-of-sample Bernoulli log-likelihood lift $\Delta \mathrm{LL}_{\mathrm{val}} = \frac{1}{m_{\mathrm{val}}}\sum_i \big[\ell(Y_i, \hat p_{\mathrm{disc}}(X_i, h_i)) - \ell(Y_i, \hat p_{\mathrm{disc}}(h_i))\big]$ ($\approx 2\delta^2$ nats).

### 3B. Multi-Stage Screening Over Huge Program Spaces ($M_{\mathrm{tot}} \sim 2\cdot 10^5$–$10^6$ Test$\times$Target Pairs)
1. **Disjoint Prime-Pool Data Architecture:** Hash every prime $r$ via `HMAC_SHA256(run_salt, r) mod 10` into **Discovery (30%)**, **Validation (30%)**, **Confirmation (40%)**; form semiprimes strictly *within* each pool so zero primes (and zero $N$) ever cross splits. Exclude $n=24$ from primary discovery (`7,750` total `top2` pairs $\Rightarrow$ $\sim 1,240$ confirmation pairs in a 40% prime pool; use $n=24$ strictly as an exhaustive finite-census algebraic diagnostic).
2. **Stage 0 (Label-Blind Pruning):** Deduplicate bitwise-identical/negated tests on 64 fixed public moduli + Discovery $X$-marginals; split tests with $\min(\bar X, 1-\bar X) \in [10^{-4}, 10^{-2})$ into a separate Rare-Certificate track and drop $< 10^{-4}$.
3. **Stage 1 (Discovery, FWER $\alpha_{\mathrm{disc}} = 0.05$ via Stratified Westfall–Young Max-$|Z|$):** Compute both per-size $Z_{p,t,n}$ and the **$1/n^2$-matched pooled statistic** $S_{p,t} = \frac{\sum_{n \in \{32,40,48,56\}} w_n Z_{p,t,n}}{\sqrt{\sum w_n^2}}$ with Neyman–Pearson weights $w_n = n^{-2}\sqrt{m_{\mathrm{disc},n}/D_{\mathrm{eff},n}}$. Calibrate exact correlation-aware thresholds via $B = 1000$ within-stratum permutations of $Y$ (exploiting heavy correlation among grammar neighbors to beat Bonferroni by $\sim 0.5$–$0.8$ in $z$). Cap promoted survivors at top $K_1 \le 64$ passing Westfall–Young $\alpha=0.05$.
4. **Stage 2 (Validation Sign-Lock & Split/Floor Audit, $\alpha_{\mathrm{val}} = 0.01$):** Re-score $K_1$ on `split_free` Validation data across all $n$; require **identical sign** to Discovery, robustness to enlarged strata $N \bmod (2^{12}\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot 7) \times \text{top}_{12}(N)$, and step-down Holm $p_{\mathrm{val}} < 0.01$. Lock surviving set $K_2$ and signs $s_{p,t} \in \{\pm 1\}$.
5. **Stage 3 (Confirmation):** One-pass evaluation of $K_2$ on Confirmation (`split_free` and `all` reported side-by-side) at one-sided Holm $\alpha_{\mathrm{conf}} = 0.01$.

### 3D. Fitting & Testing Polynomial ($C n^{-k}$) vs Exponential ($A 2^{-\beta n}$) and Subexponential ($A e^{-\gamma\sqrt{n\ln n}}$) Decay
- **Estimation (Signed Linear Profile WLS — NEVER $\log|\hat\delta|$ OLS):** On Confirmation (`split_free`, $n \in \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{fit}} = \{32,40,48,56,64,80,96\}$, omitting $n=24$), let $y_n = s_{p,t}\,\widehat{\mathrm{adv}}_{\perp,\mathrm{conf}}(n) \sim \mathcal{N}(\delta(n), \sigma_n^2)$ with known weights $w_n = 1/\sigma_n^2$.   - $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{exp\text{-}bar}}$ (known physical barriers `NE1`/`cov`/`SQUFOF`): $A 2^{-\beta n}$ ($A \ge 0,\; \beta \ge 1/6$);
  - $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{exp\text{-}free}}$: $A 2^{-\beta n}$ ($A \ge 0,\; \beta \ge 0.02$);
  - $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sub}}$ (`CFRAC`/`ECM` $L_N[1/2]$): $A \exp(-\gamma\sqrt{n\ln n})$ ($A \ge 0,\; \gamma > 0$).
- **Exact Curvature Identifiability Warning (`spec_audit_numbers.json`):** At $m = 10^8$ per size on $[32,96]$, true $1/n^2$ achieves noiseless $\Delta\chi^2 = \mathbf{105.2}$ against $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{exp\text{-}bar}}$ ($\beta \ge 1/6$: $82.0\%$ power, $0.0\%$ FP), **but only $\Delta\chi^2 = 3.36$** against unconstrained $A 2^{-0.064 n}$ and **$1.14$** against $A e^{-0.24\sqrt{n\ln n}}$ because $n^{-2}$ and $2^{-0.064 n}$ differ by at most $8.1\times 10^{-5}$ across a $3\times$ span in $n$! Extending confirmation with one anchor at **$n = 128$** triples the log-concavity lever arm.
- **Pre-Registered 4-Gate Criterion for a "Persistent Polynomial Lead":**
  1. **Large-$n$ survival (`C1`):** $z_{\mathrm{conf}}(80) \ge 3.0$ **and** $z_{\mathrm{conf}}(96) \ge 3.0$ on `split_free` instances.

  4. **Split-exclusion invariance (`C4`):** Removing `any_split_or_budget_factorable` shifts $\hat\delta_{\mathrm{conf}}(n)$ at $n \ge 64$ by $< 20\%$.

## 4. Adversarial Leakage & Artifact Checklist (8 Concrete Failure Modes)

1. **Direct & Disguised Accidental Splits (`L1`):**
   - Intermediate non-units $\gcd(v, N) \in \{p, q\}$ in modular division, Euclid/CF steps, EC projective $Z_m$, or LLL vectors; **plus silent splits** where `gcd` was not called (e.g. square form $Q_{2i} = r^2$ in CF of $\sqrt{kN}$ splitting $N$ via $\gcd(A_{2i-1}-r, N)$ — in `leakmap` E2, 28.7% of $n=32$ and 8.1% of $n=40$ instances had a square form at $2i \le 64$, creating $|z| = 34.1$ fake archimedean hits that vanished to $|z| \le 1.6$ once excluded!).
   - **Mandatory Budget-Matched Split Oracle (Harness-Side):** Flag every instance `split_any = gcd_hit OR fermat_split OR pm1_pp1_split` where: (i) any intermediate residue $v \not\equiv 0 \pmod N$ has $1 < \gcd(v, N) < N$; (ii) $|q - p| \le 4 B_{\mathrm{step}} N^{1/4}$ (Lehman/Fermat within test step budget $B_{\mathrm{step}}$); (iii) $\exp((\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^*) \mid M_{B_{\mathrm{step}}}$ or $(p \pm 1)$ is $B_{\mathrm{step}}$-smooth when `G1`/`G6` uses $M_B$. Report **all** statistics both `split_excluded` (primary) and `all_instances` (secondary).
2. **Archimedean Size Smuggling & Bit-Length Truncation (`L2`):**
   - Rejection condition `N.bit_length() == n` on full $(2^{b-1}, 2^b)$ couples $(p,q)$ across $pq \ge 2^{n-1}$; `top2=True` fixes top 2 bits of $p,q$ and caps $q/p < 4/3$; `isqrt(N)`, `frac(sqrt(N))`, CF $a_0$, and LLL determinant directly encode $N$'s top bits (`newfamily` saw $|z|=5.69$ on `nc_ded5_bits` from size!).
   - **Fix:** Use $U_{\mathrm{PNT}}(p \mid N)$ for top/ratio targets, stratify every test on 10 top bits of $N$, and verify $|z| < 3$ on negative size controls (`top16(N)`, `frac(sqrt(N))`).
3. **Sorting Asymmetry $p = \min(p,q)$ (`L3`):**
   - Induces $\Pr[\mathrm{bit}_j(p_{\min}) = 1] = 1/2 - 2^{-(j+1)}$, forces $p < \sqrt{N}$, and turns $(D \mid p_{\min})$ on $(D \mid N) = -1$ into an archimedean orientation target rather than a genus target, plus boundary pinning at small $N$ (e.g. $N < 3089^2$ in $b=12$ `top2` forces $p \in \{3079, 3083\} \equiv 3 \bmod 4$ with probability $1$!).
   - **Fix:** Always evaluate stratum-centered $\widehat{\mathrm{adv}}_\perp$ (never raw $\Pr[X=Y]-1/2$), drop the extreme $0.1\%$ tail near $B_{\mathrm{lo}}^2$ and $B_{\mathrm{hi}}^2$, and score every target on **both** $p_{\min}$ and random $p_* \in_R \{p,q\}$.
4. **CRT / Fiber-Floor & Chebyshev Finite-Prime-Set Imbalance (`L4`):**
   - At $n=24$ (`top2`), exact $\Pr[\varepsilon=+1 \mid N \equiv 1 \bmod 4] = \mathbf{0.4597}$ and $\Pr[\mathrm{QR}_2=1 \mid N \equiv 1 \bmod 8] = \mathbf{0.4592}$; at $n=32$, $\Pr[\mathrm{QR}_2=1] = \mathbf{0.5194}$ (`eps` §3: $z = 12.3$ against hardcoded $1/2$!). Also $p+q \equiv N + 1 \pmod \ell$ whenever $(p-1)(q-1) \equiv 0 \pmod \ell$, and $p \bmod 2^k$ interacts with $N \bmod 2^k$.
   - **Fix:** Stratify on $N \bmod (2^8 \cdot 3 \cdot 5)$ (or $N \bmod (2^8 \cdot \ell)$ for mod-$\ell$ targets); verify exact degeneracy $V_{\mathrm{MH}} = 0$ on pure floor controls `N mod 256`, `(N | 3)`, `(N | 5)`.
5. **`next_prime` Prime-Gap Bias (`L5` — Verified in `spec_audit_numbers.json`):**
   - Using `gmpy2.next_prime(random_odd)` instead of uniform rejection weights each prime $p$ by its preceding gap $g_p = p - p_{\mathrm{prev}}$. On 16-bit `top2` primes, `next_prime` shifts $\Pr[p \equiv 1 \bmod 3]$ from **$0.5010 \to 0.4763$** ($-2.47\%$ bias $\Rightarrow z > 20$ on $2\cdot 10^5$ pairs!) and inflates $\mathbb{E}[\log_2 \mathrm{smooth}_{13}(p-1)]$ from $5.181 \to 5.220$ via Hardy–Littlewood singular-series correlation between preceding prime gaps and $(p-1)$ small divisors!
   - **Fix:** **Strictly ban `next_prime` in instance generation**; use uniform odd bit-string rejection (`instances.py::random_prime`) with BPSW / 50-round MR.
6. **PRNG State Coupling, Seed Reuse & Float53 Precision Loss (`L6`):**
   - IEEE-754 `float64` has **53 significand bits**: at $n \in \{56, 64, 80, 96\}$, `math.sqrt(N)`, `N * alpha`, and `log(N)` silently truncate the low $n - 53$ bits of $N$ (creating kinked behavior right between $n=48$ and $n=56$!). Also Mersenne Twister (`random.Random`) must never share state between instance generation, GL masks, and test coins.
   - **Fix:** Use exact integer `gmpy2.isqrt_rem` and $\ge 192$-bit fixed-point / `mpfr` arithmetic for all archimedean primitives (`frac(sqrt(kN))`, `frac(N*alpha)`, $U_{\mathrm{PNT}}$); isolate RNGs via domain-separated SHA-256 / ChaCha streams; enforce AST isolation test forbidding operational code from importing harness/factoring modules.
7. **Small-$n$ Cross-Split Prime & Semiprime Leakage (`L7`):**
   - Drawing independent seeds without partitioning primes leaks prime-level main effects across Discovery/Validation/Confirmation at $n \le 48$ ($805\times$ prime reuse at $n=32$, $63\times$ at $n=40$, $4.7\times$ at $n=48$).
   - **Fix:** Enforce hash-partitioned disjoint prime pools $P_{\mathrm{disc}} \sqcup P_{\mathrm{val}} \sqcup P_{\mathrm{conf}}$ + dyadic cluster-robust $z$.
8. **Algebraic Tautology (`FROZEN` / `CLOSURE` / Degenerate CRT Polynomials) (`L8`):**
   - `prim` Lemmas FZ and CL proved 38–61% of raw nested-gate zeros come from small-exponent integer identities (e.g. $(a^r+1)^r = a+1$ when $a^r = -(a+1)$ and $r$ odd; $(2^N+1)^N = 1$ impossible for prime partners by `Lemma P`), collapsing depth-$d$ gates to depth-1 `(M)`/`(A)` alignments.
   - **Fix:** Pre-screen grammar templates symbolically at $r \in \{-2,-1,0,1,2\}$ and check residual advantage after masking small-order `SMALL` events $\mathrm{ord}_p(x) \le 64$.
