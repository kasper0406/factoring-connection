## Population and endpoints

Use the hatch_h1c public form generator and archived six forms per size for calibration only. At 64, 80, 96 bits set K=64. Choose B independently per size from 256,384,512,768,1024,1536,2048,3072,4096,6144,8192 to minimize the distance between six and the total number of matched witnesses in the six archived boxes; break ties toward smaller B. Keep the selected B for every new form. Generate 192 new unplanted semiprimes and balanced forms per size using the archived fixture and form functions with random.Random(202611252000 + 10000*bits + rep), rep=0..191. Use only public N and Q operationally, no tuning, retries on empty boxes, or selection by witness count. If generation fails, record why; distinctness is audited. Record occupancy and unrestricted and matched witness counts. Calibration targets a mean of one matched witness, not exactly one on every form.

For apples-to-apples first-witness search, all timed arms stop at their first distinct primitive positive point (u,v) with fully B-smooth norm and a good-prime divisor d in (2K²,4K²], matching the archived forced sieve exactly. Count every failed form and unfinished sibling actually explored in the pooled numerator. Exact norm verification uses the archived primorial test, followed on positives by exact factorization/recomposition and a divisor-range check. Archive-matched branch supplies d and can use it as the certificate. Return at most one witness per modulus. This measures fresh verified smooth representations, without asserting useful class-group rank.

## Methods and charging

- Spatial tree splits the longer side, u on ties, midway, down to 4 by 4 leaves. On equal-area siblings, rank by the *excess* of the exact primitive pair count over the primitive population of that child times sum over unordered distinct good split p,q of 4/((p+1)(q+1)). Good means odd split and coprime to the leading coefficient and discriminant. Use exact comparisons. DFS visits the best child first and backtracks; no pruning, full accounting for failures. In a leaf, scan v then u until success. Seeded public fair coins shared with the control break score ties and determine the order for unguided DFS. Count entered tree nodes, dead leaves, and candidate tests. A scored sibling whose subtree is never entered still incurs its scoring cost.
- Implement the exact score in two ways. (A) Explicit pairs: build one packed mask per good prime from its two slope lattices, with an explicit gcd primitivity mask. Intersect each unordered pair once for the entire box, charge even empty intersections, and distribute its set bits into point weights. (B) Transposed: for every prime-root incidence at a primitive point increment the pair count by the previous number of distinct primes there. Both build summed-area tables, including primitive populations, before search. They must give identical scores and traversal. This explicitly charges the box-area preprocessing and permits a more competitive shared implementation than processing all pairs anew per child. No true smoothness label or matched divisor is available to either scorer.
- Unguided DFS is the identical tree, leaf verifier, and tie order, with no score or area-table setup. Primary external comparator is the archived hatch_h1c row-by-row matched divisor sieve, same root/modulus generator, d window, per-branch v scan, deduplication, and verifier, adapted to stop immediately on its first hit. Its branches are CRT slopes; spatial nodes are reported separately. Independent full-box truth via the archived full_lattice_sieve and direct matched eligibility. On archived forms validate the original matched path and compare all box/child scores against direct pointwise arithmetic. On every held-out form validate outcomes against truth and the identity of both score implementations.
- Single process. Method order shuffled deterministically on every form. No fitted parameters. Primary total work is sum of process CPU nanoseconds per hit, end to end, including each method's own setup, pair intersections, prime/root progression setup and visits, primitivity, table construction, exact arithmetic and unsuccessful searches. Record wall time too. Shared public-fixture and input prime list generation, scoring-blind independent audit, and offline bootstrap are excluded from each arm. Record disaggregated work counters for audit; heterogeneous operations are not summed with arbitrary weights.
- Report pooled nodes or CRT branches per fresh relation, CPU per fresh relation, paired time ratios and spatial branch ratio against unguided. Percentile 95% intervals from 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples of whole held-out forms within size (seed 252611). Include empty forms in each resample. For trend report largest/smallest ratio-of-ratios and its stratified bootstrap interval; compare all three point estimates. Descriptive intervals, no asymptotic inference. If interrupted at the two-hour compute cap, report completed balanced prefixes, with incomplete cases explicitly identified.
