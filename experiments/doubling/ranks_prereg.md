# Direct Gaussian-coordinate correlation check

The registered binary association tables are already known. Before evaluating any continuous-rank associations, test the same Jacobi and residue-bucket indicators against the ranks of the signed integers a_p and a_q and of |a_p|/sqrt(p) and |a_q|/sqrt(q), within each of the three sizes and restricted to 1,1. Use midranks for ties. Standardize columns and use the absolute Pearson correlation of the indicator with the ranked outcome. For 8191 joint row permutations in each size, use seed 202611260+size and the maximum statistic over all indicator/outcome pairs. A follow-up pass needs family-wise permutation p<0.01/3 at all sizes with a consistent endpoint and direction. Retain each observed maximum, its endpoint, and its corrected p-value.
