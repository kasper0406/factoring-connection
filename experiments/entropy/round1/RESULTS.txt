Discovery and confirmation

The two-sided discovery cutoff was 1.44676 × 10⁻⁸ across 3,456,000 comparisons. All 482 surviving test–target pairs came from the 24-bit matched sample with flagged products included. Every 24-bit product split inside the 256-step Fermat budget. The clean analysis therefore starts at 32 bits, and it produced zero discovery hits. The flagged fractions among the 200,000 primaries per size were 100%, 85.873%, 37.685%, 11.5265%, 3.003%, 0.704%, 0.031% and 0.0005%, in increasing size order.

All 482 survivors were tested in both holdouts at all eight sizes. None reached the corrected one-sided cutoff of 2.59336 × 10⁻⁵ in even one of the four clean 80- and 96-bit holdout cells. None met the registered polynomial fit and separation criteria against an exponential. No asymptotic rate can be inferred from this result.

Controls and sensitivity

The planted 1/n control cleared the discovery cutoff on all eight unfiltered discovery samples. Matching and split filtering reduced power. At 96 bits, the clean matched middle-bit view retained 49,725 discovery rows. Exact positive-direction discovery power was 15.32% for 1/n and 9.53 × 10⁻⁹ for 1/n². The observed 1/n advantage was +1.139 percentage points, with a pointwise exact 95% interval of [+0.699, +1.580]. A separate power calculation gives 80% power for a 96-bit 1/n² leak at about 900 million eligible rows under the same discovery cutoff. This experiment leaves effects on that scale unresolved.

The shuffled-label screen had zero corrected discoveries. One low-bit sanity comparison failed Holm correction across 216 planned checks.
