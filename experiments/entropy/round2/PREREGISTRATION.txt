Entropy round 2. Prospective local registration, before all round-2 sampling, timings and outcomes.

Population and stages. Use top2 primes in [3*2^(n/2-2),2^(n/2)), sorted p<q. Partition primes by a fixed keyed SHA-256 hash into D/V/C (30/30/40 percent). Seeds, orientation and controls use separate public domains under ent-c-R2-v1. Uniform prime tables or uniform odd/wheel rejection with certified primality. Reject equal factors. D at 40,48,56 gets 240,000 unique semiprimes per size; V gets 120,000 unique per size. Exclude round-1 sampled products from new data. C uses separate pools, independent draws with replacement for exact agreement intervals; report within-C repeats. Fresh C anchors at 40,48,56 have 250,000 rows each, n=64 gets 40 million, n=80 gets 40 million, n=96 gets 220 million. C64 scores all locks, C80/96 the first five. Generate sealed confirmation inputs prospectively if useful. Only the fixed controls can run if no organic test locks. No replacing failed locks with best losers. Priority under the three-hour wall cap: D/V and C64, then C96 then the optional C80 volume. Fixed-count prefixes or chunks chosen by runtime alone if necessary.

Target definitions. Evaluate Gemini's normalized top/ratio bits k=1..4 using its exact continuous-PNT conditional CDF (192-bit MPFR). Exclude sqrt(N)-max(lo,N/hi)<16 log N for PIT. Trim outer .1% of normalized public product range at each end for the centered screen. For the random-factor twin use the two-sided CDF U/2 for the lower factor and 1-U/2 for the upper. Use eight SHAKE-derived GL masks per size with bit zero and the top max(4,ceil(log2 n)) cleared and at least four low-half bits, four dense 16-bit PIT-GL masks, three interior bits, low bits 1..4, six same-sign genus symbols (-1,2,-3,5,-7,-11), specified cubic and quartic fibers, and mod3/mod5 half-set targets. Score smaller-factor and coin-oriented twins; duplicate target definitions still count toward correction. Low-bit and public-floor controls are diagnostics. The primary universe is the specified top2 distribution. An empirical PIT check tabulates within-size-bin target means against actual primes.

Grammar and cost. Implement G1 multi-term, towers, inverse, SLP and polynomial-extension coordinates; G2 negative-discriminant prime forms and NUCOMP/NUDUPL powers exceeding sqrt(N); G3 multiplier CFs and structured Euclid, with real infrastructure jumps when implementable; G4 quadratic Frobenius/discriminant, division-polynomial and cusp proxies; G5 small Coppersmith/Taylor and GAR lattices; G6 projective elliptic ladders with N-coupled, shifted and compressed scalars; G7 dense mask parities, digit sums, carry and automaton readouts. Use exact modular/integer arithmetic. Budget each primitive family variant on the same label-blind n=64 panel, with projection and instrumentation, median of three batch passes. Skip variants above 50 microseconds per input; for derivative XOR/majority tests conservatively sum distinct parent costs, with a small readout allowance. Cap at 20,000 canonical tests including unary bit/parity/Jacobi/normalized projections and cross-family Boolean combinations. Deduplicate constants, equal and complemented vectors on a separate fixed 512-modulus panel. Freeze grammar after those timing and witness checks, before observing labeled scores. Record implemented, excluded and omitted variants.

Split audit. Mark proper gcd on semantic primitive residues, inversion denominators, CF states/convergent square witnesses, form coefficients, reduced lattice vectors and ladder intermediates. Deterministically handle singularities, never use factors to construct a prediction. The common external budget checks Gemini's |q-p| <= 4*64*N^(1/4), bounded Lehman k<=32 with eight offsets, and either factor +/-1 being 64-smooth. The discovery clean screen uses the union of retained primitive flags plus external flags. Validate the candidate on both full-battery-clean and path-clean data when available; lock/confirmation uses the candidate path plus common external budget. Report inclusive and clean sizes, reason counts and intersections. Library-internal temporaries beyond explicit algorithm steps are outside the observable trace and must be identified.

Discovery inference. Group by N mod 3840 and normalized product bin among 1024; groups below eight pool into (N mod 256,64 size bins). Pair rows at random within each resulting stratum, independently of features and labels; drop odd leftovers. For eligible, clean pairs, D_i=(X1-X2)(Y1-Y2) estimates twice within-stratum covariance. Test direction with exact two-sided sign randomization on nonzero D_i; under the sharp within-pair exchangeability null signs are fair conditional on discordance. Use Bonferroni across catalog x all targets x four screens (three sizes plus pooled sign count); this replaces Westfall–Young and controls the stated testing family without estimating an extreme permutation tail. Exclude marginal frequency below 1e-4 in either direction and track rare predictors. Pooled sign count weights individual informative pairs equally. Recompute the generic statistic for shortlisted pairs, inspect the full stratified covariance/MH score, and require a dyadic endpoint-cluster sensitivity at n<=48 for any triggering single-size screen (for pooled screens use summed size variances). Require agreement of sign and multiplicity-adjusted threshold under both randomization and conservative max(MH,dyadic) normal sensitivity. Promote up to 64 by smallest adjusted evidence, ties by catalog and target index. Independent V requires the frozen sign and one-sided Holm .01 on the pooled matched sign score, plus same directional pooled centered association with finer strata (public mod 430080, 4096 bins, sparse fallback as above). Lock first <=20 by validation evidence. Preserve both lock manifests, including an empty list.

Confirmation and controls. Record signed raw agreement advantage, exact 95% Clopper–Pearson interval, oriented direction, centered covariance and MI with Miller–Madow correction, clean and inclusive. For organic centered advantage give a conservative finite-sample interval via simultaneous exact binomial cell/mass bounds within public strata (the CP interval on raw agreement is not an interval on a covariance). Use a one-sided .01 Holm family per large size on locks. Fixed controls: middle-bit predictions synthesized by independent Bernoulli agreement of 1/2+1/n, 1/2+16/n^2, 1/2+1/n^2 on actual sampled rows. Compute realized estimates and exact power at .01, and conservative first Holm cutoff where applicable. Fixed negative sentinel uses a dense parity of N versus bit 1 of an independently chosen random factor on N=3 mod 4 (exactly fair given N). Controls never enter the lock family. Shuffle diagnostic on discovery uses within-matched-pair random swaps. For controls fit signed confirmation agreement (the direct planted parameter); for organic locks fit centered signed confirmation effects with their sampling SE. Use Gaussian signed profile likelihood with nonnegative amplitude, poly k in [0,6], exponential beta>=1/6 and separately beta>=.02, and exp(-gamma sqrt(n log n)). Report 2 log likelihood ratios and refitted parametric-bootstrap p-values (2,000 replicates) under each exponential. Do not use chi-square calibration of non-nested models or log absolute effects. Lead requires split-clean z>=3 at 80 AND 96, Holm pass, barrier 2LLR>=9.21 and bootstrap p<=.01, poly fit chi-square <= 99% quantile at fitted-size-count minus 2, compatible low/high slopes (difference <=2 SE) and <20% inclusive/clean relative difference at high n. Distinguish weak exponential when free/sub alternatives are not separated by >=6.63. When a prerequisite fails, record it.
