Entropy round 2 — files, measurements and replay

The local registration is PREREGISTRATION.txt with registered.utc and prereg.sha256. The measured grammar was frozen in grammar.sha256 at grammar.utc. The independent validation lock was hashed in locks.sha256 at locked.utc. POWER_ADDENDUM.txt, power_addendum.sha256 and power_addendum.utc specify the eligible-row top-up before its generation or inspection of confirmation effects. The original results and the augmented results are both retained.

Replay on the original arm64 macOS machine with ./run_round2.sh. It uses ../.venv/bin/python, Homebrew GMP/OpenSSL, Apple's CommonCrypto, and the repository environment's FLINT and PARI shared libraries. Sampling and files

For each n both primes lie in [3*2^(n/2-2),2^(n/2)). HMAC-SHA256 assigns primes to pools using the first two big-endian digest bytes of the little-endian 64-bit prime, with cutoffs 19661 and 39322. Sampling is uniform from complete certified tables up to n=64, with exact wheel-based rejection and FLINT word primality above 64. The C pools are disjoint from the two other pools at each size. The low-size discovery and validation products are unique and round-1 products are excluded. C uses independent draws with replacement. identities*.json records actual product repetition and prime reuse. Supplementary 96-bit sampling rejects the entire independently sampled pair unless the two residues modulo 8 coincide, exactly enforcing N mod 8 = 1 for odd primes.

data/{n}_{D,V,C}.u64 and data/{C,S}{n}_{chunk}.pairs contain two little-endian uint64 columns, p and q, sorted in each row. Each large chunk contains one million rows. C is the original confirmation cohort and S the supplemental cohort. The .budget companion contains one byte per row. Bits 0 to 5 denote the common proximity bound, bounded Lehman split, either p−1 or q−1 being 64-smooth, either p+1 or q+1 being 64-smooth, overlap with round 1, and the public edge trim. The independent arithmetic audit compares the optimized bounded checks to direct trials. The .locked record contains four little-endian uint32 values per row: public stratum, catalog-prediction mask by lock rank, path-split mask by lock rank, and orientation/control bits. The .hash files accelerate duplicate auditing; candidate hash collisions are resolved against the full prime pairs. The .nodes files support two-way prime-cluster residual calculations. Keep the source factors for rerunning new tests.

Grammar and discovery

results/timings.tsv gives all three instrumented runs and inclusion decisions by variant. results/catalog.tsv lists all program IDs, costs and definitions. For unary programs a is the primitive ID in variants.txt, b is its output slot, and c indexes READS in grammar.py. For XOR and majority programs a,b,c reference unary program IDs. ring.hpp, algebra.hpp, geometry.hpp and variants.hpp contain the primitive implementations. The operational state is N-only; a factor-aware Trace callback records divisibility for the separate split mask. A singular inverse returns the deterministic all-zero readout for that primitive. FLINT NUCOMP/NUDUPL and PARI infrastructure operations expose coefficients after composition and reduction. FLINT LLL exposes input and reduced bases. Internal library temporaries are opaque. Independent PARI, python-flint, affine elliptic, polynomial, CF and elementary checks are in audit_arithmetic.py. The NUCOMP reference and arithmetic API are documented by FLINT and PARI at https://flintlib.org/doc/qfb.html and https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/ref-stable/Arithmetic_functions.html.

The CF count slot packs the count of even-index Q values with squarefree core dividing 210, plus 128 times the odd-index count. Index 1 is the first denominator after a0. Other slots are the final denominator, maximum next partial quotient, and running quadratic character modulo 11. The real infrastructure variant composes reduced principal-cycle forms and brackets the fourth-root distance with baby steps using 192-bit multiplicative distance tracking. The imaginary forms use discriminant −4kN and exponent N or 2^ceil(n/2), exceeding sqrt(N). The implemented G4 outputs are discriminant/Frobenius proxies, including the degree-four 3-division polynomial for 37a1. The G5 reductions use 3–5 dimensional Coppersmith shift and GAR bases. G6 includes Montgomery and independent 37a1 Jacobian formulas. Unimplemented extensions include higher torsion division polynomials, extension-field elliptic ladders, Manin-symbol homology, general Babai residuals and a full X_0(N) point count. The slower square-root-distance jump and AKS7 were implemented and timed.

results/targets.json lists masks and targets. Labels use the 192-bit exact CDF under the continuous PNT density, an approximation to the discrete prime distribution. PIT targets and random-factor twins are defined in the registration. Results for same-sign genus twins coincide mathematically. The dyadic variance combines scores from both endpoints, including when a prime switches from smaller to larger factor. Discovery uses within-stratum matched sign randomization, multiplicity across all 5,280,000 declared comparisons and the conservative MH/dyadic sensitivity. This is strong Bonferroni control under the registered sharp exchangeability null. It uses equal-weight pooled informative pairs in place of the proposed Westfall–Young simulation and Neyman weighting. Rare tests are identified by their discovery marginal; the same family cutoff applies to both tracks.

results/D{n}_screen.npz contains counts[test,target,positive-or-negative], ones by test, eligible_pairs by target, and matched_pairs for that size. For a given pair the matched estimate is (positive−negative)/eligible_pairs. The last target column is the shuffled control. D*_flags.json gives all-battery exclusions. empirical_catalog.tsv reports equivalence up to complements on the full discovery inputs. promoted.tsv, validation.tsv and locks.tsv preserve promotion, validation and ranks. validation_views.tsv and validation_loglift.tsv give extra full-battery/path-clean and independent prediction diagnostics. pit_calibration.tsv and pit_empirical_by_bin.tsv compare with discovery-trained empirical CDFs. floor_sanity.tsv records known within-floor degeneracies.

Confirmation and inference

results/confirmation.tsv contains one row per cohort, size, view, lock/control, including actual counts, signed agreement, exact equal-tailed agreement bounds, centered covariance, conservative simultaneous-cell bounds, standard errors, conditional and marginal Miller-corrected MI in bits and one-sided Holm tests. n00,n01,n10,n11 refer to the original catalog orientation and label. signed_agreement and signed_centered use the frozen direction; adv and centered precede that orientation. all and clean share the same target eligibility and public trim. clean additionally excludes the common budget and that candidate's path splits. The archived C*_strata.npy and S*_strata.npy store integer [measurement,view,public bin,cell] sufficient counts. The matching masks.npz contains measurement keys. Each of the 1,048,576 fine bins encodes the sorted unit-residue index modulo 3840 multiplied by 1024 plus the normalized size bin. Sparse bins pool into the registered 64-size-bin floor with residue modulo 256. The confirmation centered estimator uses the complete covariance table; the matched estimator above is the discovery screen. Prime reuse and estimated design effects are in cluster_sensitivity.tsv and identities*.json.

The raw agreement interval is an exact 95% Clopper–Pearson interval on P(X=Y)−1/2. The distribution-free centered bounds simultaneously cover fine-stratum cell masses and remain valid after sparse pooling; these bounds can be wide. conditional_exact.tsv gives the exact fixed-margin randomization tail at 80 and 96 from the convolution of hypergeometric distributions, evaluated with numerical exponential tilting. Its additional centered-effect interval assumes a common conditional odds ratio across strata. That model assumption is unnecessary for its sharp-null p-value. The lead rule uses registered MH/Holm tests. power.tsv uses each test's actual eligible sample size. The reported binomial power for organic rows is a benchmark for a planted agreement leak of 1/n² on that sample size. The rare-certificate variance of an organic centered effect can be much smaller. Listed organic one_sided_p values test centered association.

scaling.tsv contains signed Gaussian profile fits, refitted non-nested parametric bootstrap tests, model adequacy and all four registered gates. A positive LR_barrier_vs_poly or LR_free_vs_poly favors the polynomial. Each statistic is twice log L(poly) minus twice log L(exp). The 2,000-replicate bootstrap p tests the corresponding exponential null. The wide_k fit and the separate *_fit_sensitivity.tsv files are descriptive checks with relaxed powers, all-size dyadic errors or the 40-bit anchor dropped. split_flags.tsv reports reasons for exclusions, including overlapping reasons. certificate_checks.tsv verifies the signed CF identity directly on rare positive rows.

In the augmented tables the primary and supplemental 64-bit cohorts are combined for all 20 locks. At 96 bits only the first four locks and the fiber-specific planted controls combine compatible N mod 8 = 1 samples. Rank 5 and the N mod 4 = 3 null sentinel retain their original 96-bit samples. Sizes without top-ups are repeated for convenience. The supplemental results reuse the original cohort and are not independent replications. The plot's left panel rescales signed advantage by 2^(n/4); its error bars use 1.96 MH standard errors. The right panel uses exact agreement intervals and the actual binomial plant.

Additional diagnostics and integrity

results/mi_null.tsv gives the fixed-margin expected plug-in MI under conditional independence for each lock and the observed excess above that expectation. The calculation sums the complete hypergeometric support in each informative stratum. A signed excess can be negative. This diagnostic supplements the registered Miller–Madow summaries. results/matched_plants.tsv measures the synthetic 1/n and 1/n² agreement on the exact same eligible, split-clean rows as each lock. The two cohorts overlap. The extra agreement_p_one_sided_exact column in the consolidated confirmation and power tables always tests raw agreement in the frozen direction.

An independent PNT quadrature check identified a round-to-nearest conversion in the initial target word. The encoder now uses the registered floor, including for random-factor twins. Every D/V comparison was replayed and results/lock_replay.log confirms identical original promotion and lock hashes. results/end_to_end_checks.json records the quadrature, shard replay and exact CI checks. results/data_manifest.tsv records the size and SHA-256 of each draw file. source.sha256 covers source and replay files. numeric_results.sha256 covers the numeric result tables, discovery counts, confirmation sufficient counts and measurement masks. RUNBOOK.txt, RESULTS.md and completed.utc accompany those records.
