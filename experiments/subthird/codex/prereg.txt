Consultant C. Written before generating fixtures or measuring a box.

Hypothesis. A public common evaluation and divided differences at two small, distinct denominators can make several non-power generators inexpensive at once. The diagnostic question is whether this creates new independent smooth relations at the aggregate norm threshold. The opposing hypothesis is that the envelope across embeddings pays for the different denominators.

Inputs. Use two balanced semiprimes per target size 60, 72, 84 and 96 bits of the special form N=A^2-2. Generate them by choosing a split prime p and a lift A of a root of 2 modulo p, and testing the exact quotient for primality. This is fixture code only. Recover A from N by integer square root in the constructor. No factor input to the constructor, no claim of a general root algorithm. Use k=Q(sqrt(2)), n=8, m=2 (nested baseline), 3, 4. Deterministically select monic P with P(b)=A by digit expansion, and Q=P+j(T-b)(T^2+1)(T-1), taking the first j and nearby base for which both absolute field polynomials are irreducible. Use the two quotients of Euclidean division by T^2+1 and T-1 as successive coordinates, using each side's polynomial. Compare with the same fields' consecutive powers at the same rank. Certify genuinely non-power projections when the span of quadratic products has dimension greater than 2m-1. Test all ternary integral coefficients in the standard basis of k.

Counts. For bit length l and d=16, B=2^ceil((l*ceil(log2 l)^3)^(1/4)). Aggregate product cap X0=3^(2d)*2^ceil(l/d); GP-height comparator X1=3^(2d)*2^ceil((2m-1)l/(2d-1)). These are declared finite normalizations, not finite-N predictions of G_r. Count low points, both-norm B-smooth points, absolute prime-ideal parity rank tagged by side, and incremental rank after quotienting by smooth rows from the union of the coordinate deletions. Deduplicate k-projective coefficient directions by minimum product norm before ranking. Log coordinate k-span of mixed smooth points. Report unrestricted and capped results. Record finite yield exponent log(1+incremental rank)/log(3^(2m)), and differences with the matched power control. Estimate no asymptotic alpha from these fixtures.

Screen passes only if on both largest fixtures at each of 84 and 96 bits, some *same* m>=3 in the divided-difference family has certified non-power projections, at least m new independent ideal rows under X0, full mixed coefficient span, and at least twice max(1,incremental rank) of its matched power control under X0. Even a pass would need growing-rank replication and an extraction argument. A failed screen rejects this design as evidence for the proposed improvement.

Theorem check. Compute the joint span of homogeneous products for each selected divided-difference pair over k until it reaches both fields; verify an integral index multiple of N using the first independent monomials or their order-basis determinant. Fail on nondivisibility or a spanning degree exceeding 2(ceil(2n/m)+1)+1. Cross-check exact box norms with repository resultants and use the repository embedding rows and exponent ledger. Retain inputs and per-instance results in scratch.
