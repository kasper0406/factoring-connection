Third experiment. The two-sided LLL screen required unequal polynomial representatives. Test the omitted possibility that two fields share the same sparse non-power polynomial recipes. Written before measuring these recipes.

On the original eight N-only fixtures, start at 1,T. For m=3 then 4 consider one monic polynomial of degree j=2,...,m+1 whose constant and linear terms are zero and coefficients of T^2,...,T^(j-1) are in {-1,0,1}. Choose the polynomial minimizing the full two-side log envelope, independently of box norms or smoothness, among extensions with both square-space dimensions >2m-1. Break ties in enumeration order; certified interval comparisons, ties or overlaps keep earlier. Keep previous selections. Enumerate and rank with the original rules, product caps and same-power controls. The hypothesis is that a small envelope can restore mixed independent rows in the ternary box without producing a power basis. The original two-largest-sizes screen governs any claim of a favourable signal. A positive rank in just one fixture is a construction of small independent rows at finite size only.
