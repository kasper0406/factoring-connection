Lemma 2. Partial threshold and fresh-rank bounds

1. A necessary small-product-space condition for threshold failure (proved).

Use absolute embeddings with conjugates counted separately. Write e=[k:Q],
J=N(c)N(c'), M_i=max_j |u_ij|, H=sum log M_i-log J and Q=sum log t_i-log J.
Let C>=1 bound the house of each letter in the fixed ternary O_k alphabet.
For any i choose m independent coefficient rows attaining its threshold.
Their determinant d is a nonzero algebraic integer. Its absolute value at
every base embedding is <=(C sqrt m)^m. The product formula therefore gives
|sigma d| >= (C sqrt m)^(-(e-1)m) at sigma=i|k. Replace column j by the
evaluations of those rows. The new determinant is u_ij times sigma d, and
Hadamard bounds its modulus by sqrt(m) t_i (C sqrt m)^(m-1). Consequently

  log(M_i/t_i) <= (em/2) log m + O_k(m).                         (1)

The single-form loss can really have order m log m. Take an invertible
zero-one matrix A with an inverse entry of size exp((m/2)log m-O(m)).
The form with coefficient column A^(-1)e_j for the appropriate j has
t<=1, since its values on the rows of A are 0 or 1. An arbitrarily small
generic perturbation makes all nonzero box evaluations nonzero and keeps
t<=2. This tests the local estimate only. Alon and Vu, Main Theorem,
https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/av1.pdf

Normalize the generator span by a pair w with unit common evaluation as
in KH, giving a space V containing 1. Powers are k-spans of homogeneous
products. Let s_h=max_b dim_k pi_b(V^h) and g_h=h(2 ceil(2n/s_h)+1).
For every positive integer h,

  Q >= (x-D log(C_k D))/g_h - (e/2) Dm log m - O_k(Dm).         (2)

Indeed V is contained in V^h and both generate the reduced product algebra.
The algebra step of KH applied to V^h puts a full side in its power
g_h/h. Undo normalization by w^g_h. The original integral degree-g_h
monomials have equal evaluations, and their k-span contains a one-sided
element with unit evaluation, the corresponding component of w^g_h.
The KH index and covolume proof in the original ideals c^g_h and c'^g_h
gives g_h H >= x-D log(C_k D). Equation (1) gives (2). This uses no
height bound for the normalization. See notes/subthird_results.md:128 and
experiments/subthird/codex/consultant_c.txt:25 in the parent repository.

At the specified balance, T is comparable to n/m, x/T is comparable to
Dm log m, and D log(C_k D)=o(x). If g_h=o(T), (2) gives Q>=x/T for
all sufficiently large x. In particular s_2/m tending to infinity suffices.
More generally, suppose even Q<x/(2T). Then (1) gives H=O_k(x/T), hence
g_h >= a_k T for some a_k>0, for every h. Since g_h <= h(4n/s_h+3),
one has s_h <= C'_k h m for all h <= a_k T/6. Balance constants are fixed
here. Also H-Q >= (1/2-o(1))x/T by KH at h=1. By (1), a positive
fraction of all D embeddings must have log(M_i/t_i) >= b_k m log m,
for some b_k>0. This is a necessary combination of slow growth and many
poorly conditioned local systems. It does not construct a linked example.

2. A direct rank charge for points with few deep cancellations (proved).

Let A_i=Cm M_i be an upper bound in the ternary box, and consider eligible
points (nonzero on both sides, unit common evaluation) with norm product
divided by J at most Y. Fix a width w>=1. Say i is deep for a if
log(A_i/|L_i(a)|)>w. The following count is uniform in the heights.

At a single embedding sort the positive values in the eligible box. As the
cutoff moves, its k-span runs through at most m different positive ranks.
For one attained rank r, write v for the first value giving that rank.
Choose r independent rows at or below v. Every point at a later value with
the same rank is in their span. Use an invertible r-coordinate minor to
solve for its coefficients. Numerator determinants have house <=(C sqrt r)^r.
The bound on the conjugate denominator determinants from (1) gives local
coefficient sizes <=(C sqrt r)^(er). Thus all values in this rank band
are in [v, r(C sqrt r)^(er)v]. Ties and jumps of several ranks cause no
problem. Each band needs O_k(1+m log(2m)/w) intervals of log-width w.
Including all ranks, there are P=O_k(m(1+m log(2m)/w)) choices at a tag.

Among points with at most l deep tags, partition by their deep set and
their interval on each deep tag. All other tags already have width w.
The number of cells is at most sum_{j<=l} binom(D,j) P^j. A cell has
envelope <= log Y + Dw after subtracting log J. To see this, compare
the maximum at each tag with the value at any one point of that cell.

Let q be the k-rank of a cell. Take a basis of eligible points in it.
Divide their paired span by one such point. The two projections generate
fields over k. Their joint algebra is the product of those fields or the
graph of a k-isomorphism. In the product case apply the KH argument with
effective degrees <=n and larger projection dimension >=q/2. After
multiplying back, the one-sided power of the normalizing point belongs to
the ambient ideal lattice and has unit evaluation. This proves the index
bound in the original fields even if the projection fields are smaller.
The ambient KH packing proof uses c,c' and the actual cell envelopes.
For q>=3 and Z=log Y+Dw it gives

  x-D log(C_k D) <= (8n/q+3) Z.                                  (3)

For the unequal-degree algebra step and the integral one-sided witness,
see codex/subthird_2_gemini.out:5 and :7. No extraction reduction is used.
In the graph case, q is at most the degree b of the largest subfield of
either ambient field k-isomorphic to a subfield of the other (over k).
This follows by projecting the normalized graph injectively to that field.

A q-plane meets the ambient ternary box in at most 3^(eq) points by
projection to q independent coordinates. Thus the union of the non-graph
cells contributes fresh binary parity rank at most

  [sum_{j<=l} binom(D,j)P^j] 3^(e max(2,8nZ/(x-D log(C_k D)-3Z))),

when the denominator is positive. For all cells include b in the maximum.
This counts candidates before any parity quotient, so products, units and
previously exposed rows can only reduce the bound. It applies inside the
confining spaces collectively, without enumerating their intersections.

For the L[1/4] cap log Y=o(x/T) take w=m and l=o(m/log x). Then
Z=o(x/T), q=o(m) in the product case, and the log cell count is o(m).
Their total fresh rank is exp(o(m))=B^o(1) when log B is comparable to m.
This includes the graph cells when b=o(m). For the sharper cap
log Y=O(sqrt(x log x)), w=O(log m) instead gives q=O_k(1) for product
cells. Widths w with 1<=w=o(m log m) likewise give the subpower bound.

3. Remaining obstruction.

The hypothesis on deep tags has not been proved for the capped box. High
rank graph restrictions can occur inside a reduced module. A bound on the
number of hyperplanes alone cannot supply a summable rank induction. Even
the fixed 2(m-1) hyperplanes a_j=+a_1 or a_j=-a_1 for j=2,...,m have
2^(m-1) distinct intersections obtained by choosing one sign for every j,
all different lines with ternary representatives. This is an incidence
example only. A full rank charge needs arithmetic input on these leaves
or their profiles. Corrected Rel-BT has a boundary error exceeding the
ternary box, as recorded in notes/hatch_results.md:64.

4. Where the divided differences sit (proved).

On a side with P(alpha)=sqrt(2), the quotient generator for a monic R is
(sqrt(2)-(P mod R)(alpha))/R(alpha), provided R(alpha) is nonzero. For
the archived rank-three family the common denominator is T^2+1. After
clearing it, the three generator numerators have degrees at most three.
At rank four the additional pole is T-1 and the numerator degrees are at
most four. Consequently the projected h-fold product span has dimension
at most 3h+1 or 4h+1, respectively, and at most the projection field
degree. Multiplying by a fixed nonzero scalar does not change dimension.
More generally take pairwise coprime monic pole polynomials with total
degree p, using 1,T and the corresponding quotients. Making Q-P divisible
by (T-b) times their product preserves the linked evaluation at b for
each quotient. Clearing the common denominator bounds s_h by h(p+1)+1.
Thus p=O(m) gives an explicit growing-rank test class in the slow-growth
regime, whenever the chosen fields and generators satisfy the original
irreducibility, independence and reduction conditions. This dimension
calculation supplies no lower bound for its number of escaping rows.
