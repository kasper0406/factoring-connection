Adaptive factor intervals | frozen before generating data

Use independent trials at 48, 72 and 96 product bits and child widths
X=floor(N^(13/50)), floor(N^(3/10)), floor(N^(2/5)) (integer roots).
Use 384 trials in each of the nine cells, without tuning or optional stopping.
Use separate Python Random streams per cell, seeded by 2026112500 + 10*i+j
for zero-based bit index i and width index j. Draw iid uniform odd half-size
integers by rejection to primes (deterministic primality for these sizes).
Sort primes p<q, require exact product bit length. Define
L=max(2^(n/2-1),ceil(N/(2^(n/2)-1))), H=isqrt(N).
Require L+2X <= p <= H-2X before drawing a uniform integer U in [0,2X).
The public parent is [p-U,p-U+2X), so both children are feasible and only
p can divide N there. Supply only N, parent start and X to the scorer.

Tangent statistic: for midpoint c=lo+floor(X/2) of each child, take all
positive standard CF convergents u/v of N/c^2 with u,v <= 2n, and include
1/1. For each pair try floor(uc+vN/c)+j for j=-4,...,4. For each nonnegative
discriminant D=t^2-4uvN take its nearest integer square k^2 (tie down).
Require at least one of (t +/- k)/(2u) to lie inside the half-open child.
A zero gap must yield an integer divisor in that child (verify by division)
to count as a witness; discard other zero gaps. The soft score is the
minimum positive |D-k^2|/(2k+1), lower is better; missing score is infinity.

Positive control and combined decision: take one centrally placed certified
Coppersmith block per child. Use the existing floorsum.lattice_block and
best_params(N,isqrt(N//2),nmax=10) capped at X; use direct division for a
singleton. Verify every hit and compare the control against the exact
planted location within the tested block. A witness of either kind chooses
its child. Without witnesses use the tangent soft score and fair coin ties.
Record primary combined accuracy, verified hits by source and overlap,
and soft accuracy conditional on both witness methods missing. Fair coin,
always-left, and largest intersection with the public feasible generator
support [L+2X,H-2X] (ties fair) are baselines. All tie and baseline coin
flips are independent of the placement.

Report by child width, with bit-size detail retained. The claim of residual
positive signal requires a one-sided exact binomial tail against 1/2 and
a one-sided exact paired sign test against the support baseline on the
same misses, both below 0.05/3 at that width. Report Wilson 95% intervals
for residual accuracy, unadjusted, and denominator. Keep decisions, sample
truth and factors for independent checking. No extrapolation from a hit at
these sizes to an asymptotic advantage at a fixed excess exponent.
