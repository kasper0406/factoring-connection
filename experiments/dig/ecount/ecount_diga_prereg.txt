Prospective protocol, before generation of count labels. Fixed curve y^2=x^3-x+1 (cubic discriminant -23).
Draw 2400 products at each exact bit length 40,60,80. Independent uniform odd half-length candidates by rejection for primality, discard bad primes, repeated primes within a size, and wrong-length products. NumPy PCG64 seed 370231.
N-only features: in each of the monic cubic f=x^3-x+1 and the monic version of psi_3=3x^4-6x^2+12x-1 over Z/N, compute u=x^N-x. For c=0,1 use Jacobi(norm(u+c),N), with norm the polynomial resultant. Record proper gcds as direct splits and exclude those rows; keep a zero feature for gcd=N. No root counts, curve orders, or factors in features.
Truth: PARI prime-field point counts, multiplied. Check M odd is impossible when Jacobi(-23,N)=-1. Check one 40-bit pair by direct character sums.
Baseline strata: bit length, N mod 24, Jacobi(-23,N). Test targets M odd, M mod 3 equal to 0, and M mod 3 equal to 1 (last two span the categorical indicators). Within-stratum centered Pearson correlation for 4 features x 3 targets pooled across bit lengths. Use 999 simultaneous within-stratum label permutations (same perm for all targets); family-wise p for max absolute correlation with +1 correction, discovery at p<=.05. Print largest correlation, test p, split counts, per-size residues. Positive control: for 20% random rows replace parity by feature 0 > 0; compare correlation on those combined labels to the max-null threshold. This is an association screen, not a search for arbitrary nonlinear functions. No additional confirmatory comparisons.
