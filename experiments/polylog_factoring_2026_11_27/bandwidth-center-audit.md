# Shorter phase windows and the record on selecting centers

Set $M=N^2$. The supplied-center evaluator splits a two-component modulus when it finds a mixed line in the band. The two successful large controls used centers planted from the CRT phases. A zero-centered search also recovered a genuine low line on $N=35$. These check the native band test. General selection from $N$ still needs a hit-rate guarantee. ## Fold the spectrum with a public stride

For a unit $t_0$, every scale $t=t_0+Nj$ is a unit modulo $N$. A line at $f\bmod M$ becomes $e_M(ft_0)e_N(fj)$. Thus a window of $L$ consecutive $j$, with the additional multiplier $e_N(-cj)$, sees the lower residue $f\bmod N$. A mixed line with local labels $a,b$ now satisfies $(f-a)(f-b)=0\pmod N$. The same translated quadratic-root search works with block width $\Theta(\sqrt N)$. A proper gcd of $f-a$ with $N$ identifies the component after the small label-difference divisors have been checked. This charges all empty blocks.

The omitted line at centered lower distance $d>0$ has average magnitude at most $N/(2Ld)$, rather than $M/(2Ld)$ in a contiguous scale window. With finite Laurent mass bound $W$ and inverse accuracy parameter $r$, use radius $B=\min(\lfloor N/2\rfloor,\lceil WrN/L\rceil)$. On a hit, compute the full CRT frequencies to evaluate the complex start phases and the geometric sums. On a miss, the known diagonal lines give the approximation. Every query scale is a unit, so no coprime-density correction is required. Squared magnitude uses $O(1+r\sqrt N/L)$ blocks times polynomial bit overhead. Unsquared magnitude uses the previous truncations and an additional $O(r^2)$ label-pair bound. [Implementation and controls](coarse_phase_window.py)

This gives a native phase average with only $\sqrt N/\operatorname{poly}(\log N)$ consecutive progression indices, compared with $N/\operatorname{poly}(\log N)$ for the contiguous-scale evaluator. The progression spans $NL$ in the original scale, outside the earlier small-scale interval $1\le t\le2N$ at this window length. A unit stride only permutes the full frequency circle. A stride with a proper gcd against $N$ splits directly. If the stride is divisible by $N$ and its quotient is a unit, it gives the same lower-circle projection up to a permutation. A stride divisible by $N^2$ folds every frequency to the same point.

## What widening costs

On the full circle of size $M=N^2$, a width-$\Theta(B)$ test visits $O(1+B/N)$ blocks. A complete cover with $B\ge N$ uses $\Theta(M/B)$ bands and $\Theta(N)$ blocks in total. With a fixed number of hidden lines, uniform random centers hit with probability $\Theta(B/M)$ for $B\le cM$ with $c<1$. Misses charge their full block count, so wider bands exchange queries for per-query work. On the folded circle substitute $N$ for $M$ and $\sqrt N$ for the block width. Complete coverage takes $\Theta(\sqrt N)$ blocks there. These are counts for uniform search and this explicit cover. An arithmetic selector or a way to certify many folded bands together could change the cost.

The earlier rational-center count also applies when the denominator bound grows. If $K|a-b|<X^2$ on balanced pairs at scale $X$, its exceptional-pair bound is $O(K^3T(T+|a-b|))$ for radius $NT$. In particular, when $T$ and the labels are polynomial in the bit length, all rational centers with denominator at most $N^{1/4}$ still hit only $N^{-1/4+o(1)}$ of the balanced prime pairs. This includes a direct conversion of a residue at a Coppersmith-size auxiliary prime into a normalized angle. One can choose the rational numerator and denominator using the full input. [Rational-center proof](modulated-phase-window.md)

An input-dependent center exists on a structured family. Suppose $vq-up=g>0$ with $u,v,g$ polynomial in the bit length and $g$ much smaller than $p,q$. Put $q_0=\sqrt{uN/v}$ and use $j=q^{-1}\bmod g^3$, which can be enumerated. For $f\equiv a\pmod{p^2}$ and $f\equiv b\pmod{q^2}$, the identity $p^{-2}\equiv u^2(g^{-2}+2vqg^{-3})\pmod{q^2}$ and inverse reciprocity yield the normalized center
$$\frac{a}{M}+(b-a)u^2\left(-\frac{gj^2+2vj}{g^3}+\frac{2v}{g^3q_0}\right)\pmod1.$$
Here $q-q_0\le g/v$. The discarded terms are $O(|b-a|uv/(g^2N))$ in normalized frequency, so the integer frequency lies in a polynomially searchable band. The reciprocal-square-root correction makes this a genuinely $N$-dependent center outside the small rational sweeps. The same promise already gives $vq^2-gq-uN=0$, so it supplies no broader coverage than a direct quadratic factor search for nearly rational factor ratios.

## Earlier selection results

- The geometric near-square prime-class shortlist factored 14 of 96 fresh inputs, versus 13 for the same number of lowest-index probes. It hit none of the 24 cases at 45–46 bits. The proved coverage bound for the tested scaling is $N^{-1/12+o(1)}$. It selects a geometric class, not a stationary Fourier center. [Class shortlist](../ellipse_disk_mod8/class_selection.md)
- The centered projective recurrence rebuilt the correct form and split all 204 controls with at most 33 count calls. Its native traversal has order $NB/\sqrt\Delta$ nodes on the growing principal branch, where $B$ is the geometric cutoff. It is an exact decoder with a charged exponential traversal. [Centered recurrence](../ellipse_disk_mod8/centered.md)
- A continued-fraction square relation gives an exact center when it supplies a mixed square root modulo $N$. Hensel lifting then gives the corresponding center modulo $N^2$. The older Pell-order work proves a nontrivial root for Blum semiprimes at the fundamental unit, with a full continued-fraction period separating the two relations. It gives no polynomial-bit construction of that certificate. [Pell-order audit](../../notes/lemmaM_results.md)
- The adaptive exponent predictor for the scaled quarter bit scored 49.9987% on 524,288 fresh samples. Carry and Euclidean feature predictors also stayed near chance on independent moduli. Those results concern earlier point predictors rather than a supplied-band search. [Exponent screen](adaptive-exponent-screen.md) · [Carry and Euclidean features](carry-feature-screen.md)
