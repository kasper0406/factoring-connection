# Arithmetic norm-splitting control

Use Gemini's norm-splitting-curve option to make polynomial-level root-count matching exact at every prime. For each fresh quartic f, write its even/odd parts as E(X,Y)=X²+c2 XY+c0 Y² and O(X,Y)=c3 X+c1 Y. Use F(X,Y)=E²−XY O² with rational root m² modulo N. Verify F is irreducible. On (X,Y)=(a²,b²), F=f(a,b)f(−a,b), two comparable-degree factors. Ordinary nearby primitive points see the identical F, alpha and small-prime roots. Match within N on the exact algebraic small-prime part, sign, and 0.20-nat algebraic log-cofactor cells. Thus the matched size difference is at most 0.20 nats. Use fit and final N separated as in the main study. Train a saturated B1 comparator and frozen-offset G2/G3/G8 models on the matched sample, and an explicitly marked oracle square-locus model. Report matched arm rates, residual lift versus B1, and measured sensitivity of each model. Positive-control records are never inputs to the real-data experiments.
