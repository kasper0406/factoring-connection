# Smoothness experiment, frozen before the first candidate or label

## Population and splits

Use deterministic balanced, distinct, certified primes to generate exact 60–110 bit semiprimes. Make two independent families, ordinary irreducible base-m cubic and quartic homogeneous polynomials, with rational side a-mb. Within each family use eight fit moduli (60,68,76,84,92,98,104,110 bits), two calibration moduli (74,98), two selection moduli (70,102), and eight final moduli (same bit grid as fit; different N). One pooled model is locked by selection and evaluated on each test family separately. No label-dependent modulus or region selection. Seeds start at 20261120.

Use y=31, B=4093. Enumerate the primitive points of each assigned full rectangle in ordinary coordinates and each special-q rectangle in lattice coordinates. Use rational q=31, already stripped as local information; a=31i+r b with r the centered residue m modulo 31. Both native horizontal ranges are [-224,223], full b=1..256 and lattice b=257..512. The eight-row b tiles with index floor((b-1)/8) modulo 4 equal to 0,1,2,3 go exclusively to fit, calibration, selection, test respectively. Both geometries occur at every stage, with disjoint physical regions even in native coordinates across stages within a geometry. Skip zero norms. Separately generate a fixed 256 earlier primitive reference points per N, b=513..768, balanced across the two geometries. References are paid history and never evaluation targets.

## Labels and allowed inputs

Compute both integer norms, remove all multiplicities at the eleven primes through 31, and label the y-rough cofactors by exact PARI factorization (bounded at B suffices). Independently verify both side labels by repeated gcd with the product of primes through B, and check local factor reconstruction. Joint target = both sides smooth. Keep exact reconstructible data, coordinates and modulus/polynomial definitions. Fit three targets, joint primary, rational and algebraic secondary.

Baseline inputs are logs of the two stripped sizes in units of log B and the two exact valuation vectors. Fit spline/piecewise logistic side models, use their product for the initial joint score, and sigmoid-calibrate scores on the independent calibration moduli. Flexible base-only controls also get these inputs and baseline scores. Geometry extras are coarsened positions/signs and the geometry indicator, including a fixed hashed coarse-cell bit. No raw low bits, evaluated norms, cofactor residues, divisor identities above y, modulus IDs, or reference coordinates or IDs enter the test features. History adds rational, algebraic and joint empirical rates from the first 4 and 16 admissible prior references in a fixed coordinate-independent order. Eligibility uses only the two positions. Require nonzero determinant and gcd(a*b_ref-a_ref*b, product of primes in (31,4093])=1, excluding identical projective classes including infinity for every forbidden prime. Stream separation and direction apply to every split. This is a test of coarse geometry and this particular history, not an exhaustive test of H.

## Predictors and locked decision

Fit histogram gradient boosting (at most 100 iterations, 15 leaves), and a small multilabel MLP (two hidden layers, at most 48 and 24 units, at most 20 epochs), on base-only, base+geometry and base+geometry+history. Use the same deterministic sample of up to 160,000 fit candidates for the flexible fits. Base side scores on fit points are out-of-modulus-fold. Calibrate each flexible score on calibration N, allowing a convex blend with calibrated logistic baseline to minimize calibration log-loss. Do not retune after testing. On selection N choose one learner and extra-feature variant by maximizing the smaller of its mean joint log-loss gains against the logistic baseline and its matched base-only learner, equal weighting of N and the two geometries. Resolve ties by fixed order boost geometry, boost history, MLP geometry, MLP history.

Primary metric is paired log-loss improvement, positive favorable, natural logs, probabilities clipped to [1e-6,1-1e-6]. Average the two geometry means within each modulus, then equally weight independent moduli within family. Report family-specific 95% t intervals and SE from the eight N-level paired differences. Give equally weighted within-N/geometry AUC and paired t intervals. A declared signal requires the locked joint predictor to beat BOTH baselines by >3 modulus-cluster SE in EACH family and one-sided exact N-level sign-flip p<=0.025 in both comparisons in each family. A zero gain fails. Geometry-specific and one-sided-target results, and unselected models, are descriptive. Sign flips assume symmetry of modulus-level differences.

## Controls and interpretation

1. Test structured feature permutation by shuffling the entire extra-feature vector within held-out N, geometry and logistic baseline score decile. Use at least 49 deterministic replicates on a fixed 2,048-point-per-N test subsample (approximately half per geometry); compare frozen selected-model joint loss against its permuted distribution. This is a conditional diagnostic, with approximate exchangeability inside score bins. If the primary rule passes, require permutation p<=0.05 in each family too.
2. Shuffle paired side outcomes independently inside each N/geometry (also shuffle within the separate reference stream), rebuild history, refit, recalibrate and reselect. Report any threshold crossing. Require this negative control not to declare a signal.
3. For a positive control, independently draw synthetic joint labels with probabilities logistic(logit(calibrated real baseline joint probability) + 0.35*s), where s is +1/-1 from the allowed coarse-cell hash bit. This implies a conditional odds ratio exp(0.70), roughly two, and is the predeclared operationally interesting effect. Use the same fit/calibration/selection/test procedure and held-out comparisons, including matched capacity. Keep real side targets as ancillary heads and use synthetic joint outcomes for reference joint history. Report detection and oracle loss scale. A failed control limits interpretation of a null. No claim to exclude smaller signals.

## Work ledger

At a fixed confirmation budget of the top 20% per N and geometry, report relation counts and relative yield versus the calibrated logistic ranking and matched base-only ranking. Use deterministic stable ties. If the signal passes, charge the upfront sieve/feature work, reference labels, scoring all rows, and confirming selected candidates; give measured timings or break-even costs. Examine yield ratios by u=max(log C_rat,log C_alg)/log B (bins <2.5, 2.5–3.5, 3.5–4.5, >=4.5) using equal within-bin confirmation budgets. A bounded lift at these sizes cannot establish an asymptotic exponent change. Record elapsed time, software, and deviations. Keep compute within approximately two hours.
